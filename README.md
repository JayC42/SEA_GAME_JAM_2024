# SUNSHINE CAN SHOP
Itch.io page: https://kaiyo-lim.itch.io/sunshine-can-shop
## Summary
A post-apocalyptic world where everyone scavenge for energy resources, you are the only shop that can sell them.
This game was made during the SEA game jam 2024 with the theme Energy Efficiency.

Diner Dash type game, sell energy cans to customers to earn money and buy upgrades
## Game controls
Spacebar: to spawn purple and orange sun.

Mouse: click on machines to produce ingredients and cans.

Mouse: click and drag on items to move them around.
